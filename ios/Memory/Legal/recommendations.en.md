# How Recommendations Work in Memory

Version of October 14, 2026. Owner: [Name: LLC "…" / sole proprietor …], [privacy@domain]. Here we explain the logic of automated suggestions, as required by the Law of the Republic of Armenia "On Protection of Personal Data".

## Where Memory suggests things

- **Shelf → "For you"**: films and books you might like.
- **Check-in response**: after you check in a film or book, one "what's next" suggestion.
- **"Similar taste"**: people whose ratings match yours.

The Feed is not sorted by recommendations: it shows posts from friends and public posts, from newest to oldest.

## What data is used

- your ratings of films and books and your "watching/reading/finished" shelf entries (from your check-ins);
- ratings from other people who have made their shelf public;
- what you have hidden from suggestions with the "Not for me" button.

Gender, age, geolocation, messages and data from other websites are not used for recommendations.

## How suggestions are made

We compare your ratings with other people's ratings: the more films and books you have in common and the closer the ratings, the higher the "taste match". A film or book appears in your suggestions if people with similar taste rated it highly and it is not on your shelf yet. Next to each suggestion you can see which of these similar people rated it. Suggestions make no decisions for you and affect nothing other than what is shown in the suggestions.

## How to turn them off and ask a human

Settings → Privacy → Personal recommendations. When they are turned off, "For you", check-in suggestions and "Similar taste" are not shown. If you think a suggestion is wrong, write to [privacy@domain], and a human will review it.
