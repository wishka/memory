# Deleting Your Memory Account

You can delete your account and all its data in two ways.

## In the app

Profile → gear → Security → Delete account, then enter your password. Your account, posts, conversations, photos and stickers are deleted immediately and permanently.

## Without the app

Write to [privacy@domain] from the email address or phone number linked to your account, with the subject "Delete account" and your username in the message. We will reply and delete the account within 5 business days.

## What is deleted and what remains

Your profile, posts, comments, PRISM, messages, stickers, photos, shelf entries, showcase products and consents are deleted. Messages you sent to other people remain with the recipients. Information that the law requires us to keep longer is kept for the period set by law and then deleted.
