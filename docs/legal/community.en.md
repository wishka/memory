# Memory Community Guidelines

Memory is a place for moments you want to remember and people you want to share them with. To keep it that way, you may not:

- **Harass or insult** people or groups, including over their appearance, origin or beliefs.
- **Incite hatred and hostility** based on nationality, religion, gender or any other characteristic; call for violence or justify terrorism.
- **Publish material prohibited by law**: drugs and their promotion, weapons, encouragement of suicide.
- **Publish explicit or shocking content**: nudity, violence, gore.
- **Harm children**: any sexualized content involving children is removed immediately, the account is blocked, and the information is passed to law enforcement.
- **Send spam or deceive others**: pyramid schemes, phishing, fake engagement, counterfeit products in showcases.
- **Impersonate** another person or organization.
- **Violate others' rights**: publish other people's photos, music or texts without permission; disclose other people's personal data (address, phone number, documents).

The same rules apply to **live streams** and their chat: the host is responsible for what they show, and a stream that breaks the rules may be stopped by a moderator.

## What to do if you see a violation

Tap "⋯" or long-press a post, comment, PRISM, message or product, then choose "Report"; in a live stream, tap the flag at the top. Reports are anonymous to the author. What you reported is hidden from you immediately; moderators usually review reports within 24 hours, and illegal content is removed no later than 3 days. You can block a person: they will not be able to message you, and you will not see their posts.

## What happens for violations

Content being hidden or removed, temporary restrictions, account blocking. If you disagree with a decision, write to [abuse@domain], and a human will review it.
